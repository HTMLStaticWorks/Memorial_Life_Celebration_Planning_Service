COMMON QUESTIONS

Memorial Planning Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about our planning process, timelines, venues, and tribute services.

How far in advance should we contact your memorial planners?
While some families reach out weeks or months in advance for anticipated celebrations, our team specializes in prompt, compassionate coordination for immediate needs within 24 to 72 hours. A single call gets our team working for you.
Do you handle memorial venue scouting, permits, and booking?
Yes, absolutely. We source, contract, and prepare unique venues including historical chapels, tranquil botanical gardens, modern galleries, private estates, and waterfront pavilions—managing municipal permits and vendor insurance requirements.
How is a celebration of life different from a traditional funeral?
While traditional funerals adhere to structured religious or solemn protocols, a celebration of life focuses on the individual's personality, passions, achievements, and unique character. We blend storytelling, favorite music, visual tributes, and uplifting gatherings tailored to their legacy.
What is included in your cinematic tribute video production?
Our tribute films are curated documentary retrospectives. Our studio team digitizes and restores photographs, edits family home video footage, synchronizes licensed music, and weaves voiceover recordings into a poignant legacy film for the service and a digital keepsake.
Will a dedicated planner be on-site on the day of the memorial?
Yes, our full-service packages include on-site memorial directors who oversee all audiovisual checks, cue speakers and musicians, manage guest hospitality, and supervise venue setup and teardown, so your family can be entirely at peace.
