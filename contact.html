Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we start planning?
While some families contact us months in advance, we specialize in respectfully accommodating immediate needs. A simple conversation is all we need to begin.
Do you handle venue booking and coordination?
Yes, we handle all logistics. We have established relationships with elegant venues across the region and will handle every detail of the booking and setup.
Can we customize the service completely?
Absolutely. We believe every life is unique, and our "Bespoke Celebration" is entirely shaped around your loved one's specific legacy and your family's wishes.
What is included in the Tribute Video?
Our tribute videos are cinematic narratives. We professionally weave together your photographs, home videos, and carefully selected music to create a beautiful legacy film.
Do you provide on-site support during the event?
Yes, our full-service planning includes our dedicated team being present on the day of the celebration to ensure everything flows seamlessly, allowing you to simply be present.
