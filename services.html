Celebration-of-Life Planning

Personalized ceremony planning that reflects the individual's values, passions, and history. We help structure the flow of the gathering to create a deeply meaningful experience.

Venue Coordination

Helping families find and coordinate meaningful spaces. Whether it's a botanical garden, an art gallery, or a quiet private estate, we ensure the setting is perfect.

Tribute Video Creation

Creating visual stories from photographs, video, and memories. Our studio editors craft cinematic tributes set to meaningful music.

Event-Day Coordination

Managing the practical details so families can remain present. From directing guests to coordinating speakers and vendors, we handle everything quietly behind the scenes.
